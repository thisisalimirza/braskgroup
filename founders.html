Why this page exists

Your Ambition, Our Resources

We know you have big ideas and the drive to make them happen. But the path from vision to venture isn’t always clear. That’s where we come in.

Brask Group is a company first — we build and run our own ventures, and everything we know about getting one off the ground we learned by doing it, usually the hard way. That’s the whole offer here: the frameworks, the introductions, and the honest read on your idea that we wish someone had given us.

You do not need a finished product, a deck, or a co-founder. You need a problem you can describe clearly and the willingness to be wrong about parts of it.

How it works

Four steps, no theatre

No application portal, no pitch day, no waiting on a committee.

  1. Tell us what you’re building

    One email. What the problem is, what you’ve made so far, and what’s currently in your way. A deck is not required and rarely helps.

  2. A real conversation

    We take the idea apart from first principles and give you an honest read — including when we think the problem isn’t the one worth solving.

  3. Build the smallest real version

    We help you scope something you can put in front of actual users quickly, and stay close while you do it.

  4. Keep going

    What support looks like after that depends on what you need — introductions, systems, a second pair of hands. We work it out with you rather than in advance.
