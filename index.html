About

One person, lots of ambition, looking for the right team.

I'm Ali Mirza — a medical student, builder, and operator. I've shipped 50+ products, published ML research, and lived on four continents. Brask Group is the structure I use to bring ideas to life.

I started Brask Group because I kept seeing problems — in healthcare, in education, in how businesses reach people — that nobody was solving well. So I started solving them myself.

But here's the thing: the best stuff gets built by small teams of people who genuinely care. I've got the operational backbone — strategy, branding, product development, outreach. What I need are people who complement that.

This isn't a big corporate thing. It's a studio. We find problems, we build solutions, and we put them in the hands of people who need them. If that sounds like your kind of work, I'd love to talk.
