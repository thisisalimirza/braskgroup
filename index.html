Founder

Ali Mirza

After college, I worked at Epic Systems — the company behind most of America's electronic health records. I saw how healthcare technology really works at scale, and where it falls short.

Now I'm a second-year medical student at UConn, building the businesses I wish existed when I was on the other side of the system. I've shipped 50+ products, written 3 books, published peer-reviewed research in machine learning and philosophy, and lived across four continents.

Brask Group is how I organize all of this. It's lean, it's focused, and it's growing because the things we build actually work for the people who use them.
