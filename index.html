Approach

Build what we need.
Ship what works.

Most of what we build starts as something we needed and could not find. We design it, ship it, and see if others need it too.

Some stay small and useful. Others find an audience and become self-sustaining. The common thread is that they are built by people who use them.

We keep the portfolio focused: healthcare education, clinical tools, and the infrastructure that supports them.
