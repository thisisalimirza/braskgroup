Software, starting with medical education.

Brask Group LLC is a Connecticut company. It makes an exam-prep app for medical students, a school-planning site, and an event seating tool, and it runs a web design studio.

Flagship

Rounds

iOS app

Learn to think through a diagnosis, not just memorize one. Rounds gives you First Aid-aligned cases for USMLE Step 1, then clinical calculators, a differential builder and illness scripts for clerkships. Free with a medical school .edu email.
