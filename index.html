Our vision

Humanity advances when people are free to build

At Brask Group, we believe humanity advances when individuals and communities are empowered to reach their full potential.

That belief has a specific first target. AI is coming to medicine regardless of how the field feels about it — training, clinical practice, and how care gets delivered are all already changing. Some clinicians are excited, many are anxious, and both reactions are reasonable: a lot of what’s being built is built by people who’ve never sat with a patient or carried a pager.

We build from the other side of that gap. Not as a general bet on technology, but problem by problem, wherever the failure mode is clear enough to build directly against. Every product and every essay is a brick in the same structure — a specific, growing answer to what the systems people depend on should actually look like.

What we’ve built

Our Ventures

A starting point, not the whole map. Each one began as a problem we could see clearly enough to build directly against.

Advancing Health

Rounds

Medical students study the way they’d use a search engine: ask, get an answer, move on. That’s efficient, and it skips the exact back-and-forth reasoning that builds clinical intuition. Rounds puts that reasoning back in — case simulations that push students to work a case the way a real patient encounter demands, rather than handing them the answer.

Live on the App Store, used daily by students across clinical rotations, and self-sustaining on its own revenue.

Advancing Horizons

My Med Stack

A discovery tool for medical training programs, built to simplify an application process that’s more opaque than it needs to be. A straightforward data and search problem, solved as one.

Advancing Society

Sitr

Originally built to solve inpatient bed management, Sitr’s core logic generalized into event seating — a clean example of how a healthcare-first problem can produce a broader product.

Advancing Agency

Byline Blogs

An automated content pipeline giving small healthcare practices — starting with direct primary care clinics — access to real SEO content without agency costs.
