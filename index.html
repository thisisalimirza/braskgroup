Who’s behind this

I’m Ali. I’m a third-year medical student at UConn, on a path toward cardiology. Before medical school I worked in software engineering and did ML research — an ECG-based deep learning project on left ventricular hypertrophy detection, among other things. I never really stopped building; I just started pointing it at healthcare.

Outside the clinic and the codebase, I read a lot of things that don’t obviously connect to either — classical political philosophy, Islamic intellectual history, geopolitics. I published a poetry collection at some point along the way. I write about all of it, loosely organized, at Side Effects.

Brask Group is just the name for the building part.
