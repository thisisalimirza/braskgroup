The model

One system. Multiple products.

We run a focused studio that builds marketing sites for healthcare companies and early-stage startups. That work funds the products we build for ourselves.

Some of those products find real users and become self-sustaining. When they do, they become the proof that brings the next client. It is not a portfolio of disconnected side projects. It is a single operating system.

We have run this model before. The previous agency funded motion design work. Brask Studio is the tighter version: clean sites, fixed scope, two weeks, flat rate. The surplus goes into products we actually want to use.
